## What the Business Does

The office provides legitimate academic support services: guidance, proofreading, formatting, statistical analysis, citation support, and consultation for students at all degree levels. It does **not** produce work on behalf of students — all services are framed as support, review, and guidance.

---

## Project Goal

Build a professional full-stack bilingual website that:
1. Presents the business and its services clearly in Arabic and English
2. Allows visitors to submit service requests via a structured form
3. Gives the admin a dashboard to manage and respond to those requests

---

## Language and Direction

- Primary language: **Arabic (RTL)**
- Secondary language: **English (LTR)**
- All user-facing content must exist in both languages
- Language toggle switches full page direction and all text

---

## Tone and Framing Guidelines

- Use: دعم، إرشاد، مراجعة، تدقيق، استشارة، تحليل، توثيق
- Use: support, guidance, proofreading, consultation, analysis, formatting, citation support
- Avoid any wording that implies completing work on behalf of the student

---

## Key Constraints

- Mobile-first responsive design
- No payment processing in MVP
- No visitor accounts in MVP — contact is handled by admin via WhatsApp/email
- Admin is a single account managed by the business owner
- Service requests are private and visible only to the admin
